Quantum optics · Morehouse

Diode laser characterization & raw bit extraction

405 nm diode laser · HBT measurement · Swabian Instruments time tagger

I swept laser drive current from 15 to 34 mA at 20, 25, and 30 °C, using a 50/50 beam splitter and two single-photon avalanche detectors. Repeated runs and laser-off baselines helped separate the measurement from setup drift and background counts.

64.14Mraw bits evaluated
50.0033%observed fraction of ones
More on the analysis and its limits

I examined detection rates, second-order intensity correlations g²(τ), and interarrival-time distributions. Held-out prediction checks stayed close to 50% for the methods tested.

These results characterize this source and extraction procedure. Bit balance and the tested baselines do not establish cryptographic security or certify entropy.

Upper-level optics laboratory manuals

Alongside the research, I wrote laboratory manuals for the optics kit for students in HPHY 300-level courses and above. The Michelson interferometry and Hanbury Brown–Twiss guides connect the physics of light and quantum optics to practical experiments, with setup and alignment instructions, detector configuration, safety guidance, data collection, and analysis. I developed them to help junior and senior physics students understand both how to run the equipment and what their measurements mean.
