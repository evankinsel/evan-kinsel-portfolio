Case study 01 / Quantum optics

M.O.R.E. Lab · Sep 2026–present

From photon arrivals
to raw random bits.

Undergraduate Research Assistant · Advisor: Dr. Wesley Sims

I conceived and led a faculty-supervised investigation of a 405 nm diode laser, asking how drive current and mount temperature affect photon-arrival statistics and timing-based bit extraction.

Using a two-detector Hanbury Brown–Twiss configuration, I measured the split optical signal with single-photon avalanche detectors and Swabian Instruments time-tagging hardware. Controlled sweeps from 15–34 mA at 20, 25, and 30 °C combined triplicate measurements, thermal stabilization, and laser-off baseline controls.

64.14M

Raw bits extracted & evaluated

50.0033%

Observed fraction of ones

Analysis & scientific limits +

I characterized detection rates, second-order intensity correlations g²(τ), and interarrival-time distributions, then evaluated next-bit prediction under held-out testing. Prediction accuracy remained approximately 50% for the tested methods.

These results characterize the measured source and extraction procedure. Bit balance and the tested prediction baselines do not establish cryptographic security or certify entropy.

I presented the work at the Morehouse Fall 2026 STEM Research Symposium and authored laboratory procedures to support reproducible undergraduate optics experiments.

Case study 02 / Magnetometry

QuantCAD LLC · Jun–Aug 2026

Restoring the measurement.
Improving the design.

Quantum Systems Engineering Intern · Chicago, IL

I restored an inherited rotational magnetometry platform by working through sensor, cabling, mechanical, and stepper-motor control faults. I isolated the acquisition and motion subsystems, resolved integration conflicts, and re-established coordinated scanning and sensor readout.

With the platform operating, I collected more than 2,500 time-aligned three-axis measurements through automated 360° scans. Python workflows supported field-vector analysis, heading calculations, statistical characterization, and comparison with models.

In related work, I improved a magnetic-field gradient design by 61%, fabricated experimental fixtures and sensor mounts, and supported thermal testing across 9,000+ measurement points.

MagPlotter

Magnetometer processing & spatial field characterization

A Python analysis pipeline that turns raw sensor CSVs into consistent engineering reports. I developed the workflow with AI assistance to reduce repetitive cleaning and plotting between measurement runs.

  • Clean logs, calculate field vectors, and evaluate circular statistics and heading drift.
  • Interpolate spatial grids with SciPy for field characterization.
  • Report peak field, uniformity, magnetic center, and 180° symmetry scoring.

02 / Embedded sensingIn development

Desktop 3D LiDAR

Embedded acquisition → live point-cloud visualization

An ESP32-based scanner connecting distance and orientation sensing to a live Python pipeline. The project explores how sensor validation and coordinate transforms shape usable spatial measurements.

  • Integrate VL53L0X time-of-flight sensing, a BNO055 IMU, and an SSD1306 OLED over I²C.
  • Prototype polar-to-Cartesian conversion and orientation-aware XYZ capture with manual pan and tilt.
  • Stream serial measurements to Open3D, apply voxel downsampling, and export .PLY point clouds.
